# The Idea — A Moldable Lead CRM

**A DigiCloudify venture** · Standalone brand (name TBD) · Concept · 12 Aug 2026

---

## In one line

**One CRM. Every industry. Shaped by you.** A mobile-first lead-management app that any small business can mold to its own workflow in minutes — genuinely free to start, and powerful when you grow.

## The idea

Most small businesses don't lose leads because they lack a CRM — they lose leads because their CRM is either too rigid to fit how they actually work, or too expensive and complicated to bother with. So they run their sales on WhatsApp, a phone's contact list, and memory.

This product is the fix: a **lead CRM that's as easy as WhatsApp but shaped like the business using it.** Think "Notion for lead management" — one flexible core, and each industry (interior design, HVAC, jewellery, real estate, education, coaching) shapes it into their own pipeline, their own fields, their own follow-up flow. Capture every lead automatically, get pinged the second one arrives, and work it to a close from your phone.

## Who it's for

Solo operators and 2-to-10-person teams who live on their phones and run sales through WhatsApp and calls — starting with **Tier-2 and Tier-3 India**, where these businesses are underserved by tools built for enterprises or for Western markets, then expanding out.

## The gap in the market

The category splits into two camps, and there's an empty seat between them:

- **Privyr** (the global leader) is beautifully designed and mobile-first — but it's **expensive per user**, its **free tier is barely usable** (you can only work your single most-recent lead), it **paywalls WhatsApp automation as a paid add-on**, it's **rigid** (one fixed shape for everyone), and it **doesn't connect to Indian lead sources** like IndiaMART or JustDial.
- **The India-native tools** (TeleCRM, Kylas, and the budget crowd) are **cheaper and integrate with Indian lead sources** — but they're **built for telecalling call-centers**, feel **rigid and clunky**, and aren't designed as a flexible, any-industry platform.

**Nobody owns the seat in the middle: flexible AND genuinely free AND mobile-first AND WhatsApp-native.** That's the opening.

## The wedge — three things the incumbents can't easily copy

1. **Moldable.** Fields, pipelines, and views are shaped by the user, not fixed by us. One product becomes a hundred industry tools. Privyr is rigid; TeleCRM is call-center-shaped — neither can follow here without rebuilding.
2. **A free tier you can actually run a business on.** Not a locked demo — a working product for a solo operator's first few hundred leads. This is the bottom-up growth engine and the sharpest contrast with Privyr.
3. **WhatsApp automation bundled in, never a surcharge.** The exact feature Privyr charges extra for is simply included in the paid plan — running on the business's own WhatsApp number.

Supporting edges: **native Indian lead sources** (the thing Privyr lacks and TeleCRM has but does clumsily), **design that punches at Privyr's level** at an India-native price, and **regional-language onboarding**.

## How it works — the loop

**Capture → Alert → Engage → Follow up → Convert.**

A lead fills a form, replies to an ad, or comes in from IndiaMART/JustDial/Facebook/Google — and instantly lands in the app, de-duplicated and assigned to the right person. That person's **phone buzzes within seconds**. They tap once to reply on WhatsApp with a personalised message, send a brochure and **see the moment the lead opens it**, and set a follow-up they'll be reminded about. Meanwhile an **auto-responder** has already greeted the lead and a **follow-up sequence** keeps nudging — stopping the instant the lead replies. Nothing slips through the cracks.

## Lead capabilities — along the lead lifecycle

The core promise is **no lead falls through the cracks**, structured across the 7-stage lifecycle: **capture → alert → organize → engage → follow up → convert → learn.**

### 1. Capturing leads
- **Multi-source auto-capture**: Native support for Indian sources (*IndiaMART, JustDial, 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing, Sulekha*) and global ad platforms (*Facebook/Instagram Lead Ads, Google Lead Forms*).
- **Web & offline capture**: Embedded website forms, shareable capture links & QR codes for business cards/shopfronts, one-screen manual entry, and bulk CSV import.
- **Automatic deduplication**: Incoming duplicate leads are automatically merged into the existing contact timeline by phone number or email.

### 2. Never missing a lead (Alerts)
- **Instant new-lead push**: Real-time push notifications to assigned agent phones within seconds of lead entry.
- **Auto-assignment**: Round-robin distribution or custom routing rules by lead source, region, or industry field (no per-lead fee).
- **Engagement alerts**: Immediate pings when a prospect opens a shared catalogue/brochure or replies on WhatsApp.
- **Follow-up reminders**: Prominently surfaced due and overdue follow-up tasks.

### 3. Organizing leads (The moldable engine)
- **Custom pipelines & stages**: Fully customizable funnels tailored to each industry's specific workflow.
- **Custom fields**: Flexible schema to track industry-specific data points.
- **Saved views**: Filtered views (e.g. "Hot leads this week", "Site visit pending") with instant search.
- **Industry templates**: Pre-configured pipelines, fields, and stages for instant setup.

### 4. Engaging leads
- **One-tap actions**: Direct WhatsApp, call, SMS, and email triggers with pre-filled numbers — no saving contacts required.
- **Personalized templates**: Dynamic message templates supporting placeholder variables like `{name}`, `{title}`, `{company}`.
- **File & page tracking**: Trackable brochure/quote links that report exact open timestamp and view activity.
- **Complete lead timeline**: Unified chronological audit log of calls, WhatsApp chats, notes, and document opens.

### 5. Following up automatically
- **Instant auto-responder**: Immediate greeting sent via WhatsApp upon lead capture.
- **Drip sequences**: Automated multi-step follow-up sequences that automatically halt the moment a prospect replies.
- **Broadcast campaigns**: Segmented bulk messaging to filtered lead groups.
- **Task reminders**: Scheduled follow-up tasks ensuring zero missed opportunities.

### 6. Converting & learning
- **Stage progression**: Smooth movement of leads through funnel stages to won/lost with loss reasons.
- **Source conversion analytics**: Attribution reports showing which lead sources generate real revenue versus raw volume.
- **Stale-lead surfacing**: Automated flagging of untouched/ageing leads before they go cold.
- **Agent performance reporting**: Metrics tracking response speed, contact rates, and agent-level conversion rates with CSV export capabilities.

## The model

**Freemium, designed to be fair.** A generous, genuinely usable free tier drives adoption from the bottom up. Paid tiers unlock the things that either cost real money to run (WhatsApp automation volume) or matter to growing teams (more members, distribution rules, team analytics, branding). **No per-lead fees, no nickel-and-diming** — the transparent contrast with Privyr's per-user-plus-add-on-plus-per-lead pricing. Pricing gets tuned against real usage, not guessed up front.

## Why this venture, why now

- **Tier-2 conviction.** DigiCloudify is built around underserved regional India — this product is that thesis as software, complete with regional-language onboarding the incumbents ignore.
- **Vertical experience becomes product.** DigiCloudify's work across interior, HVAC, hospitality, jewellery, education, and tech turns directly into credible, ready-made industry templates — content, onboarding, and acquisition in one.
